# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as maintainers and contributors of the 8thGuard Open Verification Toolkit pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
* Keeping security, privacy, and honest evidence boundaries front and center
* Focusing on verifiable facts and reproducible test cases rather than personal attacks

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Publishing others' private information, wallet attribution without lawful basis, or personal data without explicit consent
* Submitting real private keys, live customer records, or personally identifiable information into public issues or pull requests

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers at **kev@aunatron.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
